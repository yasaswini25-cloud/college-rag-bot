COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
TRAINING & PLACEMENT CELL POLICY 2026

CHAPTER 1: PLACEMENT REGISTRATION & ELIGIBILITY
Section 1.1: Student Registration
1. All final-year students seeking campus recruitment assistance must register with the Training & Placement (T&P) Cell by July 15, 2026.
2. Minimum eligibility for standard campus placement drives is a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 6.5 or above with no standing/active backlogs.
3. Students must achieve at least 85% attendance in mandatory pre-placement training modules (Aptitude, Soft Skills, Technical Mock Interviews).

Section 1.2: Placement Offer Tiers & Dream Policy
The T&P Cell operates a tiered recruitment policy:
1. Tier 1 (Core & Service Companies): CTC up to Rs. 7.0 LPA.
2. Tier 2 (Dream Companies): CTC between Rs. 7.1 LPA and Rs. 14.0 LPA.
3. Tier 3 (Super Dream Companies): CTC above Rs. 14.0 LPA.

Policy Rules:
- A student securing an offer in Tier 1 is eligible to apply for Tier 2 (Dream) and Tier 3 (Super Dream) drives.
- Once a student secures a Tier 2 (Dream) offer, they are eligible only for Tier 3 (Super Dream) companies.
- Securing an offer in Tier 3 represents the maximum entitlement; the student is considered fully placed and is de-registered from subsequent campus placement drives to provide opportunities for peers.

Section 1.3: Code of Conduct & Offer Acceptance
1. Selected candidates must accept or decline an official job offer within 48 hours of declaration.
2. Failure to attend an interview after shortlisting without prior intimation will result in a 2-drive debarment.
