COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
OFFICIAL ACADEMIC REGULATIONS 2026

CHAPTER 1: ATTENDANCE RULES & REGULATIONS
Section 1.1: Minimum Attendance Requirement
Every student is required to maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in aggregate across all registered theory and practical courses in a semester to be eligible for appearing in the End Semester Examinations (ESE).

Section 1.2: Condonation of Attendance Shortage
1. A student with attendance between 65% and 74.9% may apply for condonation on genuine medical grounds or for representing the college/university in authorized sports, cultural, or technical events.
2. The application for condonation must be submitted along with valid medical certificates/duty slips endorsed by the Head of the Department (HOD) to the Dean of Academic Affairs within 7 working days of returning to campus.
3. A condonation fee of Rs. 1,500 per subject must be paid upon approval.
4. Students with aggregate attendance below 65% are strictly NOT eligible for condonation and will be detained in that semester, requiring them to re-register for the courses in the subsequent academic year.

CHAPTER 2: GRADING SYSTEM & EVALUATION
Section 2.1: Credit System and Scale
The institution follows a 10-point grading system based on relative grading:
- Grade O (Outstanding): 10 grade points (Marks >= 90%)
- Grade A+ (Excellent): 9 grade points (Marks 80% - 89%)
- Grade A (Very Good): 8 grade points (Marks 70% - 79%)
- Grade B+ (Good): 7 grade points (Marks 60% - 69%)
- Grade B (Above Average): 6 grade points (Marks 50% - 59%)
- Grade C (Average): 5 grade points (Marks 45% - 49%)
- Grade P (Pass): 4 grade points (Marks 40% - 44%)
- Grade F (Fail): 0 grade points (Marks < 40%)
- Grade AB (Absent): 0 grade points

Section 2.2: Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA)
1. Internal evaluation constitutes 40% of the total course marks, comprising 2 Mid-Term Tests (20 marks), Quizzes/Assignments (10 marks), and Course Projects/Labs (10 marks).
2. End Semester Examination constitutes the remaining 60% of total course marks.
3. A student must score at least 35% in the End Semester Examination and 40% overall (Internal + External) to pass a course.

CHAPTER 3: BACKLOGS AND ARREAR CLEARANCE
Section 3.1: Arrear Examinations
1. Supplementary examinations for odd and even semester backlogs are conducted twice a year in June and December.
2. A student may carry forward a maximum of 4 backlogs into the final (4th) year. Students with more than 4 active backlogs will not be permitted to register for final year capstone projects.
3. Maximum duration permitted to complete a 4-year B.Tech degree is 6 consecutive academic years.
