COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
ADMISSION GUIDELINES & ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA 2026-2027

CHAPTER 1: UNDERGRADUATE ADMISSIONS (B.TECH)
Section 1.1: Eligibility Criteria
1. Candidates must have passed 10+2 or equivalent examination with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry/Computer Science with an aggregate minimum of 60% marks (55% for SC/ST/PwD candidates).
2. Valid score in JEE Main, State CET, or the College Entrance Aptitude Test (CEAT 2026) is mandatory for merit-based admission.
3. Age limit: The candidate must be at least 17 years old as of December 31, 2026.

Section 1.2: Admission Quotas & Seat Matrix
1. Merit Quota (Government/State Seats): 60% of total intake filled through centralized state counseling.
2. Management Quota: 25% of total intake allocated based on CEAT percentile and 10+2 scores.
3. NRI/International Student Quota: 15% of total intake reserved for foreign nationals and NRIs.

Section 1.3: Required Documents for Verification
At the time of document verification, candidates must produce original and two sets of self-attested copies of:
- 10th and 12th Grade Marksheets and Passing Certificates
- Entrance Exam Rank Card & Admit Card
- Transfer Certificate (TC) and Migration Certificate from previous institution
- Character and Conduct Certificate
- Category/Caste/Income Certificate (if claiming reservation)
- Aadhaar Card / Passport identification proof
- 6 recent passport-sized color photographs

Section 1.4: Fee Refund Policy
1. Withdrawal requested 15 days before the commencement of classes: 100% tuition fee refund with Rs. 1,000 processing deduction.
2. Withdrawal requested less than 15 days before classes start: 90% tuition fee refund.
3. Withdrawal requested up to 15 days after classes start: 80% tuition fee refund.
4. Withdrawal requested up to 30 days after classes start: 50% tuition fee refund.
5. No refund is granted beyond 30 days of formal academic session commencement.
