COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
HOSTEL & CAMPUS RESIDENCE RULES AND FEE STRUCTURE 2026

CHAPTER 1: HOSTEL ACCOMMODATION & FEES
Section 1.1: Room Categories and Annual Charges
1. Single Occupancy (AC with attached bathroom): Rs. 1,40,000 per academic year.
2. Double Occupancy (AC with attached bathroom): Rs. 1,10,000 per academic year.
3. Double Occupancy (Non-AC with common bathroom): Rs. 85,000 per academic year.
4. Triple Occupancy (Non-AC with common bathroom): Rs. 65,000 per academic year.
5. Refundable Caution Deposit: Rs. 10,000 (payable once at the time of hostel admission).

Section 1.2: Mess & Dining Services
1. Annual Mess Fee: Rs. 48,000 (includes 4 meals daily: Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, and Dinner).
2. Special vegetarian and non-vegetarian menus are offered.
3. Mess timings:
   - Breakfast: 7:30 AM to 9:00 AM
   - Lunch: 12:30 PM to 2:00 PM
   - Evening Snacks: 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM
   - Dinner: 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM

CHAPTER 2: HOSTEL REGULATIONS & CURFEW
Section 2.1: Entry & Exit Timings
1. Campus In-Time / Night Curfew: 9:00 PM on weekdays and 9:30 PM on weekends.
2. Biometric attendance is recorded nightly between 9:00 PM and 9:45 PM by Hostel Wardens.
3. Night Out / Leave Passes: Students must submit an e-gate pass request via the Student ERP at least 24 hours in advance with parent/guardian OTP approval.
4. Unauthorized absence after 9:30 PM will attract a disciplinary fine of Rs. 1,000 for the first infraction, and parent summons for repeated offenses.

Section 2.2: Prohibited Items
Cooking appliances (electric kettles, induction stoves, heaters), alcohol, narcotics, smoking, and gambling are strictly forbidden within hostel premises with zero tolerance.
