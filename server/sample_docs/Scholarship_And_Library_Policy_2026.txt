COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
CENTRAL LIBRARY & SCHOLARSHIP POLICIES 2026

CHAPTER 1: CENTRAL LIBRARY GUIDELINES
Section 1.1: Operating Hours
1. Working Days (Monday - Friday): 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
2. Weekends & Public Holidays: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
3. During Semester Examinations: 24/7 Digital & Reading Hall access is provided.

Section 1.2: Book Borrowing Limits & Overdue Fines
1. Undergraduate Students (B.Tech): 4 books for a duration of 14 days.
2. Postgraduate Students (M.Tech/MBA): 6 books for a duration of 28 days.
3. Faculty Members: 10 books for the full semester duration.
4. Overdue Fine: Rs. 2 per book per day for the first 7 days, and Rs. 5 per book per day thereafter.

CHAPTER 2: INSTITUTIONAL SCHOLARSHIPS
Section 2.1: Merit-Based Scholarships
1. Founder's Excellence Award: 100% tuition fee waiver for top 3 rank holders in each engineering department with CGPA >= 9.5.
2. Dean's Merit Scholarship: 50% tuition fee waiver for students securing CGPA >= 9.0 in the preceding academic year.
3. Sports Excellence Scholarship: Up to 75% fee concession for students representing the state or nation in recognized sports competitions.

Section 2.2: Means-Cum-Merit Support
1. Financial aid of up to 40% tuition fee waiver is granted to students from economically weaker sections (family annual income < Rs. 3,50,000) maintaining a minimum CGPA of 7.5.
2. Applications must be submitted to the Student Welfare Office before September 30 annually.
